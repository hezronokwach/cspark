# **Contents**

# **1\. What Changed in This Revision**

This revised plan replaces the first draft (which assumed a private research consultancy) after reviewing the documents shared: the CSPARK Constitution, the 2020–2025 Strategic Plan, and Dr. Alando Walter's profile. Three things now shape the site differently:

* CSPARK is a registered Non-Governmental Organisation (NGO Co-ordination Act, 1990; Reg. No. OP.218/051/19-325/11713) — not a private consultancy. This changes both the tone (mission-driven, not sales-driven) and the required content (governance, registration, safeguarding, transparency).

* The website is needed for a specific grant application. Funders check an NGO's website as part of due diligence before they even read the proposal, so several sections below exist purely to satisfy that check — they are marked “Grant requirement” throughout this document.

* The Strategic Plan and Constitution supply real content — vision, mission, values, thematic goals, board structure, and named experts — so several checklist items below are already answered and just need client confirmation, not drafting from scratch.

Pulled directly from the Strategic Plan — confirm these are still current or edit them:

**Mission:** To link spatial planning to social justice through research, advocacy and capacity development.

**Core values:** broad-based stakeholder participation and teamwork; integrity in research and dealings; respect for gender, cultural and socio-economic diversity; upholding the best interest of society; financial and social accountability to partners and society.

One-line positioning statement (edit freely):

Geographic focus mentioned in the Strategic Plan: Kisumu, Nairobi, Nakuru, Eldoret, Wote, Mombasa, Kisii, Siaya, Makueni, Uasin Gishu, and the Lake Victoria basin — confirm current priority counties.

## **4.7 Transparency & Accountability — Grant Requirement**

This is the page funders look for specifically during due diligence. Even a simple version at launch substantially strengthens the grant application.

* NGO registration certificate (Reg. No. OP.218/051/19-325/11713) — image or PDF.

* Tax compliance / PIN certificate.

* Most recent annual report or activity report (even a short 4–6 page one, per current best practice for NGOs).

* Most recent audited accounts, or a statement that annual audits are conducted (per the Constitution's audit clause).

* Safeguarding policy — many funders now only fund organisations that have one in place; a short published policy is a concrete, fast win before the grant deadline.

* Data protection / privacy notice for anyone submitting information via the website (Kenya Data Protection Act).
